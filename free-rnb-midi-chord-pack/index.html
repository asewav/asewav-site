02 / In your DAW

How to use a MIDI chord pack without sounding copied.

  1. 01

    Audition the movement first

    Listen to the chord motion before choosing drums or a lead sound. Notice the bass path and which notes stay connected.

  2. 02

    Change the sound

    Try the same MIDI with piano, Rhodes-style keys, pads, bells, or layered textures until it fits the mood of the track.

  3. 03

    Edit the voicing

    Move notes up or down an octave, delete notes that crowd the vocal space, and adjust timing so the loop feels like your session.

  4. 04

    Build around the pocket

    Add bass, drums, melody, counter-melody, and arrangement only after the chord movement already feels good on its own.
