02 / Why MIDI

Why producers use MIDI chords for R&B.

MIDI gives you the notes without locking you into one sound. You can try a progression on piano, Rhodes-style keys, pads, bells, or layered textures, then move notes around until the loop leaves space for the vocal.

That flexibility matters for R&B because the emotional feel often comes from small changes: a bass note that walks down, a top note that stays connected, a ninth that softens the chord, or a suspended sound that waits before resolving.

04 / Workflow

How to build a beat from an R&B MIDI progression.

  1. 01

    Start with the chord feel

    Choose a progression and listen for the emotional direction before adding drums, bass, or lead melodies.

  2. 02

    Pick the right sound

    Audition piano, keys, pads, bells, or layered instruments until the chord color matches the record you are making.

  3. 03

    Shape the bass movement

    Let the bass support the chord path instead of only following root notes. Smooth bass motion is a big part of the R&B feel.

  4. 04

    Leave space for the song

    Edit note density, octave placement, rhythm, and voicing so the progression supports drums, vocals, and arrangement.
