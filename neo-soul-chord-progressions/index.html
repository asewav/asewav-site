Quick answer

Neo-soul progressions need color and connection.

  • Use 7ths, 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths for richer emotion.
  • Keep common tones between chords so the loop feels smooth.
  • Add slash chords when the bass needs a more musical path.
  • Let tension resolve instead of adding random complexity.

01 / The sound

What gives neo-soul chords their feel?

Neo-soul harmony often starts with familiar progressions, then changes the emotional weight through voicing. A plain minor chord can become a minor 9th. A major chord can open into a maj9 or maj13. A dominant chord can carry suspended tension before it resolves.

The goal is not to make every chord complicated. The goal is to make the chord movement feel intentional. If the top notes move smoothly and the bass tells a story, even a short loop can feel musical.

Add color tones

Use 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths when a plain triad or seventh chord feels too direct.

Use gospel movement

Try passing diminished color, suspended dominant movement, and bass notes that step into the next chord.

Leave room

Neo-soul does not need every chord stacked high. Space, timing, and sound choice matter as much as the chord name.

02 / Progression ideas

Neo-soul chord progressions to try.

Use these as starting points, not rules. Try changing keys, moving the bass, or deleting notes until the loop fits your artist, tempo, and sound selection.

Warm major color

Ebmaj9 - Dm7 - G13 - Cm9

A smooth major-to-minor pull with a colorful dominant turn.

Minor pocket

Fm9 - Bb13sus - Ebmaj9 - Abmaj9

Suspended tension makes the change feel softer and more vocal.

Bass movement

Cm9 - Bb13/D - Ebmaj9 - Abmaj9

The bass note creates a lift before the progression settles.

Late-night loop

Am9 - D13sus - Gmaj9 - Cmaj9

A mellow two-five motion that works well on keys, pads, or Rhodes-style sounds.

03 / Before and after

The neo-soul feel can come from one small rewrite.

Before

Eb - Cm - Ab - Bb

The progression is usable, but the chords may feel too plain if every change lands as a basic block.

After

Ebmaj9 - Cm11 - Abmaj9 - Bb13sus

The same broad motion now has more warmth, softer tension, and a smoother path into the turnaround.
