What is ASE WAV?

ASE WAV is the official site for producer-focused R&B harmony resources, MIDI packs, and chord guides, including Modern R&B Chords for Producers and the free 10 Smooth R&B Progressions + MIDI pack.

The full bundle includes a 124-page guide, 439 MIDI files, 25 R&B progressions, and 50 chord starters.

Can I try the ASE WAV approach before buying?

Yes. The free pack includes 10 Smooth R&B Progressions + MIDI, two PDF guides, bass movement, and producer notes.

Do I need sheet music to use the free pack?

No. The free pack is built for producers and includes MIDI files plus practical PDF guidance.
